## Preregistered measurement

The first presentation and first submission of each task are the only eligible
opportunity/answer. Re-entry or retry is practice. Count answers require exact
integer equality; supported Possible/Certain/Absent answers require exact typed
equality. Correct and wrong have no partial category. Unsupported, skip, no
answer (including exit) and unclear are distinct. Help, visible analysis,
details, reveal, aid and retries are sticky: lowering support restores no
unaided status. Record explicit learner prior exposure as none/seen/unknown.
App-local absence is insufficient to prove external novelty. Unknown, reset,
missing, migrated, incomplete or damaged histories never establish novelty.

Per-task success requires an independently supported correct first answer,
no assistance and known no previous exposure, including outside the app.
Record own-words explanation and clarity verbatim, separately from correctness.
Never convert either to strategic mastery or fabricate ordinary-game mechanism
observations. Report exact counts, never a percentage pass/mastery threshold.
Use separate denominators: presented opportunities; answered; supported scored;
correct/wrong; skip; unclear; unanswered; help; assisted; repeated; exposure
unknown; eligible first unaided answers and their correct count.

Target Delayed at 7–14 calendar days. Record both actual session timestamps,
time zone and actual elapsed seconds/days; do not assume interval compliance.
The learner may skip, end or answer unclear at any time. Stop on broken
reconstruction, an answer leak, unexpected aid, missing durable save, wrong
build/task version or invalid evidence. Record stop reason and triage every
defect. Feasibility means every attempted opportunity has a traceable outcome
or explicit skip/stop and all defects are triaged; it does not require correct
answers. One maintainer as learner is formative, with no human expert,
telemetry, efficacy, causality or publication claim.
