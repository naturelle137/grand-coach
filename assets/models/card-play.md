# Model card: card-play network

`card-play.gcnn` is the first trained network of the learned engines
(epic #67, issue #73). It decides card play, Kontra and Re; bidding, Hand
or pickup, Drücken and the announcement stay with the Classic fallback
(D-07). It is experimental: Classic stays the default.

## Data

Strong self-play generated by `nn-train generate` with engine commit
`99c91300dee7` (ISkO with Kontra/Re, three Strong seats):

| Run | Seed | Games | Data SHA-256 (over shard hashes) |
|-----|------|-------|----------------------------------|
| `strong-s73-10k` | 73 | 10,000 (complete) | `1f5f82f2…d91fb04d` |
| `strong-s74-10k` | 74 | 7,590 of 10,000 (wall-time cap; complete shards only) | `1c290ba8…be454d8c` |

Examples are the card-play decisions of every seat, split by deal:
440,242 train, 54,292 validation, 53,820 test; 4.4 % of them offer
Kontra or Re. The data are not in the repository; the full hashes are in
`card-play.json`, and the commands below regenerate them.

## Training

Targets: the policy imitates Strong's chosen card; the value is the mean
of the seat's final Seeger-Fabian points and the expected points of the
chosen option; the option heads learn Strong's expected points
(squared error) and win chance (cross-entropy) for every legal card when
the teacher's analysis was complete. Adam, learning rate 0.001, batch
256, 30 epochs on the CPU, seed 0; the shipped weights are those of
epoch 10, the one with the lowest validation loss. Sizes were chosen by a
short tuning run on 2,020 games (widths 128–1,024, two learning rates,
soft targets, value mixes; differences of at most 2.6 points of
agreement).

```sh
cargo run --locked --release -p nn-train -- generate DATA/strong-s73-10k --deals 10000 --seed 73 --cpus 16 --workers 8 --minutes 480 --max-mb 2048
cargo run --locked --release -p nn-train -- generate DATA/strong-s74-10k --deals 10000 --seed 74 --cpus 16 --workers 8 --minutes 224 --max-mb 2048
cargo run --locked --release -p nn-train -- train OUT DATA/strong-s73-10k DATA/strong-s74-10k --hidden 512 --epochs 30 --minutes 120
```

Generate with commit `99c91300dee7` (records depend on the engine) and
train with commit `bf17a5678616` or later as long as the training format
is unchanged; with the same complete shards CPU training gives the same
file bit for bit: three runs with trainers built at three commits
(`f8efa4849f7d` with uncommitted changes outside training, `4dca9398a9bd`,
`bf17a5678616`) wrote identical files; `card-play.json` is the manifest of
the last. A run
interrupted by the wall-time cap of `generate` keeps a different set of
complete shards; the data hashes in the manifest say which.

## Measured

Test split (53,820 decisions): the policy picks Strong's card in
75.6 % of decisions; the best card by the option scores is Strong's in
59.4 %; root mean squared error 45.3 points for the value and 48.4
points for the option scores.

Arena (`calibrate`, validation suite, 200 deals × 6 games each, Kontra/Re
on; pre-play by the shared rules of thumb; mean difference of
Seeger-Fabian points per seat-game with 95 % interval). B is this
model; A is the same recipe without option heads, trained for the
comparison and not shipped:

| A | B | Deals | A − B | 95 % interval |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A (no option heads) | Easy | 0–199 | +2.94 | [−1.41, +7.29] |
| B | Easy | 0–199 | +6.85 | [+2.77, +10.93] |
| B by option scores (`nnq:`) | Easy | 0–199 | +4.05 | [+0.43, +7.67] |
| B | A | 0–199 | +4.19 | [−0.63, +9.00] |
| B | Easy | 200–399 | +6.71 | [+2.06, +11.37] |
| B | Medium | 0–199 | −12.46 | [−16.97, −7.96] |
| B | Strong | 0–199 | −18.13 | [−22.40, −13.85] |

All 8,400 games finished without an illegal action (the arena aborts on
one); 24 of each 1,200 games on deals 0–199 and 6 on deals 200–399 were
thrown in.

Latency per decision: 0.14–0.15 ms median, at most 0.20 ms p90 and
0.32 ms maximum per card-play or Kontra decision on one CPU thread
(`latency 20 nn:card-play.gcnn`).

## Limits

- Weaker than Medium and Strong in card play; stronger than Easy (measured above).
- Imitation of Strong: it inherits the teacher's double-dummy bias
  (perfect defenders in every sample) and its habits (Kontra in almost
  every offered decision).
- Pre-play is not learned: bidding and declaring come from the fallback.
- Contracts in the data (17,471 games played, 119 thrown in): 14,875
  Farbspiele, 2,584 Grand, 12 Null; 41 Hand games, 4 Ouvert. Null,
  Hand and Ouvert play is barely trained, because Strong's rules of thumb
  rarely declare them.
- Option estimates are model estimates, never exact values, Monte-Carlo
  samples or a reliable comparison; the coach must not present them as
  such.
- The value head overfits the shared game result (train RMSE 23.0
  against 45.3 on test).
